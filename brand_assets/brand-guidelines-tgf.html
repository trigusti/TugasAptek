06 — Usage Rules
Do & Don't
✓ Do
  • Use Plus Jakarta Sans exclusively for all text
  • Use Energy Yellow as the dominant brand color
  • Keep sufficient contrast — dark text on light, light on dark
  • Maintain clear space around the TGF monogram
  • Use Coral as a highlight or CTA accent only
  • Keep layouts clean with generous white space
✗ Don't
  • Mix other typeface families into the design
  • Place the logo on busy or low-contrast backgrounds
  • Use all three brand colors at equal visual weight
  • Distort, rotate, or add effects to the monogram
  • Use yellow text on white — always pair with a dark surface
  • Overuse Electric Sky — it's an accent, not a base color
